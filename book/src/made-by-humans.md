# Oxiplate is made by humans

Oxiplate is built by humans;
mostly by [Brandon Frohs](https://19.codes/) ([@0b10011](https://github.com/0b10011)).

Known or suspected AI changes will be rejected.

This includes, but is not limited to:
code, documentation, and images.

If merged changes are found or suspected to have been generated by AI,
they will be reverted and remade by a human,
and the account that submitted them will likely be blocked.
